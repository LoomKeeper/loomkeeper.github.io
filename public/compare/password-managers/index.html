Loomkeeper and password managers: different jobs, one plan

If you're planning for the unexpected, a password manager is probably already part of the picture. It's a good start. But "my family could get into my accounts" and "the right people receive what I meant for them" are two different goals. Here's how the tools compare. We only describe what each product's own documentation says.

What password managers offer for emergencies

Bitwarden: Emergency Access. You can appoint trusted emergency contacts who can request access to your vault. You choose whether they get view-only access or full takeover. A request is granted when you approve it, or automatically once a wait time you set has passed. The feature is available on premium and paid organization plans.

Source: https://bitwarden.com/help/emergency-access/ (checked Nov 11, 2026)

1Password: Emergency Kit. 1Password gives you a PDF with your account details and a place to write your account password. It suggests storing a printed copy safely and giving a copy to someone you trust, like your spouse or someone in your will.

Source: https://support.1password.com/emergency-kit/ (checked Nov 11, 2026)

These tools are about access. The person you trust gets into your vault and finds what they need.

What Loomkeeper does

Loomkeeper is about delivery after your passing:

  • A Vault for important files. Files are encrypted on your device before upload and kept in one-way cold storage. You assign people (Loomkeepers) to each folder, so each folder reaches the right people.
  • Afterlife Messages. Record a video, voice note or written message for one recipient, delivered on a date you choose or a set time after your passing.
  • A confirmation step before anything is released. Either your Verification Contacts confirm your passing by a majority of the votes cast, with a 60-day period in which we try to reach you before anything is released, or a Trustee you've chosen reports it.
  • Loomkeeper is designed to release your content only after your passing has been confirmed.

Which do you need?

Often both. Keep using your password manager for sign-ins, and look at its emergency options for situations while you're alive. Use Loomkeeper for the files and words you want specific people to receive after you're gone.

Frequently asked questions

Can I store passwords in Loomkeeper?

You can store account credentials in your Vault for the people you choose to receive after your passing. Because the Vault is one-way, you can't look them up yourself later, so keep using a password manager day to day.

Does Loomkeeper replace a password manager's emergency access?

No. Loomkeeper is designed to release your content only after your passing has been confirmed.
