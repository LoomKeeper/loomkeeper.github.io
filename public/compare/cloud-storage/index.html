Your cloud storage after you're gone: what each option really does

Cloud storage keeps your files safe and easy to reach while you're here. What happens afterwards depends on the provider, and on whether you've set anything up. Here's what the biggest services say in their own help pages, and how Loomkeeper approaches the same problem.

Google: Inactive Account Manager

You can choose up to 10 people to be notified, or to receive some or all of your account data, after your Google Account has been inactive for a period you set. Google judges activity from signals like your sign-ins, My Activity, Gmail use and Android check-ins. Trusted contacts get an email with a link to download the data you chose to share.

Source: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3036546 (checked Nov 11, 2026)

Apple: Legacy Contact

You can add one or more Legacy Contacts who can request access to data in your Apple Account after your death. To do that, they need the access key you generated and your death certificate. Legacy Contacts can't access some data, including purchased media and passwords stored in iCloud Keychain. If you add more than one, any of them can individually make decisions about your data, including deleting it.

Source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/102631 (checked Nov 11, 2026)

Dropbox

Dropbox's help article for families suggests first checking the synced Dropbox folder on the person's computer. To request access from Dropbox itself, it asks for documentation that includes a government-issued ID and a valid court order, and says access can take time and isn't guaranteed.

Source: https://help.dropbox.com/account-settings/access-account-of-someone-who-passed-away (checked Nov 11, 2026)

How Loomkeeper is different

Loomkeeper isn't general cloud storage. It's built for one job: making sure the right things reach the right people after your passing.

  • You choose who gets what. Assign people to each Vault folder, and record Afterlife Messages for one recipient each.
  • Release follows a confirmation step, not inactivity. Verification Contacts vote, and the majority of votes cast decides. We also try to reach you for 60 days before anything is released. Or a Trustee you've chosen, such as an attorney or executor, reports your passing.
  • Loomkeeper is designed to release your content only after your passing has been confirmed.
  • One-way by design. Files are encrypted on your device before upload. After upload you can't open or download them yourself. You can request a specific file back, with identity verification and a fee. Keep your own copies of anything irreplaceable.

Use them together

Set up Google's and Apple's legacy settings if you use those services; they're free and built in. Use Loomkeeper for the documents and messages you want delivered deliberately, to specific people, after your passing is confirmed.
